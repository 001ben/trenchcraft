# Verification

Eleven deterministic tests cover all eight ISO directions, actual world-space boom/arm/swing/curl movement, the alternate pattern, neutral behavior, arm-driven scoop/dump, ground resistance, bucket capacity, soil conservation, off-plot and over-height dumping, trench scoring/backfill, track travel/pivot, corrupt-save rejection and save round trips. The gravity test releases the controls, saves/reloads airborne soil, and verifies it becomes a ground pile without losing volume. Earlier version 1 saves migrate to version 2.

`node tools/browser-check.mjs` drives the actual game with keyboard inputs through scoop, lift, swing and discharge. It verifies saved bucket contents and a resulting mound, cab view, in-session pattern switching and ISO defaults on reload, track travel and two simultaneous touch pointers. It checks cancellation, phone portrait, landscape, tablet and small-phone layouts, then exercises reset cancellation/confirmation. Fresh entry and reload start in cab view, Dig mode and ISO, without a blocking menu. A physical scoop runs before ever opening the guide. Tab switching resumes quietly; an explicitly opened guide remains open. Screenshots are saved under ignored `.local/`.

`node tools/model-check.mjs` compares the rendered Blender cutting point with domain kinematics through 90 articulated poses and checks turf removal/backfill. The corrected model's maximum error was approximately 0.000000091 m. A 1024×768 Windows/Edge sample measured 2.2 ms p95 for simulation plus render submission, 104 draw calls and 110,212 triangles. This is a small desktop submission sample, not a sustained phone GPU benchmark.

`node tools/bucket-check.mjs` captures empty, quarter-full and full scoop close-ups plus soil discharging before impact. It also verifies track shoes move in the correct direction for forward travel and in opposing directions for a pivot. Images were inspected for bucket opening direction, visible soil growth and the cutting-lip discharge location.

ISO directions were checked against Kubota's U10-5 operator manual (printed pages 38–41), and the rear-facing scoop and bent boom against Kubota U17 photographs. Source links are in README.md. Production type-check/build and local browser checks pass.

A complete human six-metre trench playthrough and real phone/iPad testing remain outstanding. This remains a simplified kinematic practice game: full bucket/body collision, real hydraulic forces, soil collapse and operator-training effectiveness are not established.

Reference-guided modeling pass: inspected side, front, chase, cab, full-bucket and phone views. The model check now also verifies fixed rocker-link lengths through 90 articulated poses (maximum error below 0.000001 m). The updated scene measured 113 draw calls and 122,800 triangles, with roughly 2–3 ms p95 desktop render submission in the sampled runs. The cab camera follows the new seated eye position; the phone chase camera has more sideways clearance around the taller canopy. The physical browser scoop/discharge, drive, touch and save checks pass with the revised asset.
